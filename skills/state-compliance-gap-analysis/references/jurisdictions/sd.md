# South Dakota — South Dakota Commission on Gaming (SDCG)

**Code:** SD · **Products:** sportsbook (retail + on-premises "advance deposit"/mobile only — **no statewide mobile**) · **Key authority:** S.D. Const. art. III, §25 (Deadwood/tribal-land gaming limit, as amended by 2020 Amendment B); SDCL ch. 42-7B (Sports Wagering); ARSD art. 20:18, ch. 20:18:35 (Requirements of Sports Wagering Operations) and ch. 20:18:36 (Advance Deposit Wagering)

## Payments-relevant requirements

### Licensing & change management

- Sports wagering is authorized **only** for licensed commercial casinos within the city of Deadwood and for tribes under tribal-state Class III gaming compacts (nine tribes) — this is a state-constitutional limit, not just a statutory choice (S.D. Const. art. III, §25; SDCL 42-7B-6). (HIGH)
- ARSD 20:18:35:02 (Internal control procedures): licensee/services-provider must submit internal controls to the Commission **at least 30 days before** sports wagering operations commence, and the executive secretary must approve them before launch. Required coverage includes: automated/manual risk management; employee access controls and segregation of duties; identifying/reporting fraud and suspicious conduct (incl. reporting unusual wagering activity to an independent integrity-monitoring provider); regulatory-compliance controls; AML compliance standards; controls for accepting wagers and issuing payouts, **with additional controls specifically for payouts exceeding $10,000**; and controls for detecting a patron structuring multiple wagers within a 24-hour cycle to evade recording/reporting. (HIGH — direct rule text)
- The Commission may require a licensee/provider to submit hardware/software to the Commission or an independent gaming laboratory for evaluation of the sports-wagering system or further data monitoring; exact certification cadence/change-tiering (cf. CO's pre-launch lab cert + annual review) is not spelled out as precisely in the sections found — confidence MEDIUM, verify current rule text.
- Annual financial audit (ARSD 20:18:35:40) and a separate internal-control-procedures audit (ARSD 20:18:35:39) are both required. (MEDIUM — titles confirmed, full text not reviewed)

### Deposits & permitted payment methods

- No SD-specific statute/rule was found that enumerates permitted deposit instruments (card brands, ACH, etc.) for sports wagering the way CO/MD do. The AGA regulatory fact sheet states SD "does not prohibit specific payment types" but **does not accept cryptocurrency** as a form of payment, and that **casinos may not offer credit to patrons** (no marker/credit-line deposits). (MEDIUM — secondary source; verify against current ARSD/SDCL text before relying on it)
- Deposits into an advance-deposit wagering account fund a balance that can only be used to **pay for bets placed on the physical premises of a licensed Deadwood gaming establishment** (or tribal casino) — this is the core constraint, not an incidental detail. "Mobile" wagering is a UI on top of an on-premises requirement, not statewide access. (HIGH)
- Account eligibility/verification and account establishment are covered in ARSD ch. 20:18:36 (section titles located: 20:18:36.06 Establishing an account, 20:18:36.07 Account application verification, 20:18:36.08 Account eligibility) — full text not retrieved in this pass; treat citations as MEDIUM/verify exact numbering (SD's own rules site renders this chapter with a "." separator, e.g. `20:18:36.17`, inconsistently with the ":" separator used elsewhere — both forms point at the same chapter).

### Withdrawals & payout timelines

- Advance-deposit account withdrawals: the licensee must honor an account holder's withdrawal request **within 5 business days**, unless the licensee has a good-faith belief the account holder engaged in fraudulent conduct or other conduct violating SDCL ch. 42-7B or the rules. (HIGH on the 5-business-day content — direct rule paraphrase; MEDIUM on exact section number, cited variously as part of ARSD ch. 20:18:36 — verify)
- Promotional/free-play credits deposited to an account must be wagered and **cannot themselves be withdrawn**; winnings from wagers made with those credits are cash and are withdrawable.
- No inactive-account maintenance/administration fee may be charged.
- No SD rule was located that sets a payout SLA for **retail/cage** winnings (contrast CO's 24-hour demand-payment rule or MD's 7-day online withdrawal SLA) — mark **not_found / LOW, verify** with the Commission or current rule text. Sports-wager tickets remain redeemable for one year after the final event; vouchers expire in 90 days (MEDIUM).

### Player funds segregation / reserve

- ARSD 20:18:35:35 (Form of reserve), authorized under SDCL 42-7B-7 and 42-7B-11(13): reserve may be held as (1) cash/cash equivalents in a U.S. bank account **segregated from the licensee's/provider's operational funds**, (2) an irrevocable letter of credit, (3) a surety bond, (4) payment-processor reserves/receivables, (5) any other Commission-approved form, or (6) a combination. (HIGH on form; the rule text located does not state a numeric floor or an explicit "reserve must cover outstanding liability" formula the way CO/MD rules do — mark MEDIUM/verify whether a liability-coverage or dollar-floor requirement exists elsewhere in the ICP submission the Commission approves.)

### Responsible gambling

- No statutorily mandated statewide self-exclusion program for commercial (Deadwood) gaming; instead, **each licensee must develop its own self-exclusion or voluntary-exclusion plan** to keep out any patron who notifies the licensee of a desire to self-exclude (AGA fact sheet, summarizing SDCL 42-7B). Tribal compacts do not require a self-exclusion program at all. (MEDIUM — secondary source paraphrase of statute; verify exact SDCL section)
- Commission may direct up to $30,000 from the Gaming Commission fund to the Department of Social Services for gambling-addiction treatment/counseling. (MEDIUM)
- Casinos may not offer complimentary alcoholic beverages to patrons (statewide gaming rule, not sports-wagering-specific). (MEDIUM)
- No statutorily mandated advertising restrictions or on-premise responsible-gaming display requirements were found (AGA fact sheet — "does not have a statutorily mandated" policy for both). (MEDIUM)
- Patron protection information required under ARSD 20:18:35 must include notice that underage gambling is a criminal offense and that facilitating a person under 21 to bet is also a criminal offense (exact subsection not confirmed — LOW, verify).

### KYC / age / geolocation

- Minimum age to participate in sports wagering (commercial and tribal) is **21**. (HIGH)
- ARSD **20:18:36.04 — "Location detection requirements"** is the operative geolocation rule for advance-deposit/mobile-style wagering: it requires confirming the patron is physically on licensed premises (within Deadwood city limits, or on the applicable tribal gaming premises) at the moment of each wager — not merely somewhere in South Dakota. This is a materially narrower gate than the "anywhere in the state" geofencing used by mobile-legal states. (HIGH on existence/citation; MEDIUM on exact operative text, which was located by section title/summary rather than full text — verify.)
- High-school events cannot be wagered on at all; college events involving any SD college/university team or athlete cannot be wagered on, and proposition bets on collegiate events generally are prohibited. (MEDIUM)

### AML overlays

- ARSD 20:18:35:33 (Anti-money laundering monitoring): licensees/providers must "develop and implement anti-money laundering procedures and policies that adequately address the risks posed by sports wagering for the potential of money laundering and terrorist financing"; the Commission may impose further requirements through the approved internal control procedures. (HIGH — direct rule text; effective 8/22/2021 under 48 SDR 014)
- The internal-control-procedures rule (20:18:35:02, above) independently requires: AML compliance standards; extra controls on payouts over $10,000; and detection of a patron structuring multiple wagers within a 24-hour cycle to evade recording/reporting — this is SD's clearest state-specific structuring/CTR-adjacent overlay beyond generic federal BSA. (HIGH)
- No separate state-level CTR/SAR filing regime beyond federal BSA obligations was identified (consistent with AGA's framing that AML compliance for SD casinos rests on federal law). (MEDIUM)

### Records, reporting & data

- Gaming Internal Control and Revenue Reporting Manual, ARSD art. 20:18, Appendix A, governs recordkeeping/revenue reporting across Deadwood gaming generally (sports wagering included). (MEDIUM — title/scope confirmed, contents not reviewed in this pass)
- Integrity-monitoring information sharing (ARSD 20:18:35:28) allows the Commission to share event-integrity information with licensees, providers, and law enforcement. (MEDIUM)
- Refused-bet logs must be kept and made available to the Commission on request (ARSD 20:18:35:14). (MEDIUM)
- Separate annual financial audit (20:18:35:40) and internal-control-procedures audit (20:18:35:39). (MEDIUM)

## Code review focus

- **Geo/jurisdiction gate defaults to wrong-open for SD.** If payment rails in this codebase are enabled per-state by default, SD needs an explicit narrower block: money movement (deposits, advance-deposit account funding, and the wager itself) must be restricted to sessions confirmed **physically on a licensed Deadwood casino's premises or an authorized tribal casino's premises** — not "anywhere in South Dakota" and never statewide. A per-state boolean (`SD: enabled`) is insufficient and would authorize exactly the statewide-mobile deposit flow SD's constitution and ARSD 20:18:36.04 do not permit. Treat any statewide-mobile SD deposit path found in code as a hard finding — illegal-market / UIGEA / Wire Act exposure, not a minor gap.
- Verify the geolocation check re-validates at time of wager/deposit against an on-premises geofence (tight radius around the licensed casino), not a state-boundary polygon reused from mobile-legal states — a shared `US_GEOFENCE` component keyed only by state code is the likely failure mode.
- Credit-line deposit paths must be hard-blocked for SD (no patron credit permitted); confirm no fallback to a generic "credit OK" default from another state's config.
- Cryptocurrency deposit/payout rails must be disabled for SD.
- Advance-deposit withdrawal SLA: confirm a queue/worker enforces completion within 5 business days of request, with the only pause condition being a documented good-faith fraud/compliance hold — not an unconditional manual-review bucket.
- Reserve/segregation: confirm operator-side ledger can produce the SD reserve composition (segregated cash in a U.S. bank vs. LOC vs. surety bond vs. payment-processor receivables) distinctly from general operating funds, and that payment-processor "reserves/receivables" claimed toward the SD reserve are traceable to an actual processor holdback, not just an internal accrual.
- AML: confirm a 24-hour multi-wager structuring detector exists and is wired to the payout path, and that payouts over $10,000 route through the extra controls ARSD 20:18:35:02 requires — a generic BSA/CTR $10k threshold reused verbatim from federal cash-transaction logic is not necessarily the same control this rule requires for sports-wagering payouts.
- Self-exclusion: since SD has no mandated statewide list, confirm the codebase's self-exclusion flow is driven by **this licensee's own plan** (block deposits/wagers on self-report) rather than assuming a state registry feed will arrive the way it might for a mandated-list state — a missing state-registry integration for SD is not itself a gap.
- Promotional/free-play credits: confirm ledger enforces "must be wagered, cannot be withdrawn directly" and that only winnings derived from them are marked withdrawable cash.
