# Engagement Shape Guide

## Objective

Provide defaults for the plan, prerequisites, risks, final audience, and follow-ons when the inputs are thin. What the SoW actually says always takes precedence over these defaults.

## Required actions

1. Classify the engagement as `discovery`, `build`, or `strategic`. Record a hybrid as its primary shape plus a secondary shape.
2. For a **discovery** engagement, including design and discovery sprints, apply these defaults:
   - **Arc:** week 0 set-up and planning; map and understand; synthesise and frame; design sprint or concept development; test, refine, and report.
   - **Critical prerequisites:** research participant access, stakeholder diaries, current-state documentation, journey data (volumes, drop-off, handling times), voice-of-customer data, and the exec playback slot.
   - **Highest-leverage risk:** access to research participants. Always write a research access plan with a fallback ladder.
   - **Final audience:** an executive or investment group. Plan around tangible outputs, such as a prototype, roadmap, architecture view, and backlog, rather than a re-walk of the findings.
   - **Follow-ons:** usability testing, build-ready specs, service design, op-model work, the next journey, and build.
3. For a **build** engagement, with embedded product and design in delivery, apply these defaults:
   - **Arc:** mobilise and onboard; backlog and design foundations; iterative delivery cadence; release and measure.
   - **Critical prerequisites:** tool and environment access, backlog or requirements, design system, product owner availability, engineering counterparts, and release governance.
   - **Highest-leverage risk:** decision rights and product owner availability.
   - **Final audience:** the product owner and steering group. Plan around working software, velocity, and outcome metrics.
   - **Follow-ons:** next release scope, adjacent product areas, and capability uplift.
4. For a **strategic** or advisory engagement, apply these defaults:
   - **Arc:** align on questions; gather evidence (interviews, data, market); form options; recommend and roadmap.
   - **Critical prerequisites:** senior stakeholder diaries, strategy documents, performance data, and a decision forum date.
   - **Highest-leverage risk:** senior availability and unclear decision owners.
   - **Final audience:** the C-suite or board. Plan around a clear recommendation, a roadmap, and the investment case.
   - **Follow-ons:** discovery on the priority options, transformation support, and maturity uplift.
5. For every shape, plan a week 0 that sends the client the prerequisites checklist at least one week before week 1. Week 0 is a checklist and a kick-off call, not a full week of effort. Log the time spent on it.
6. Mark every value taken from this guide `DEFAULT`.

## Done when

- The engagement shape is recorded.
- Every default used in either pack is marked `DEFAULT`.
- The plan includes a week 0 with a prerequisites send date.
