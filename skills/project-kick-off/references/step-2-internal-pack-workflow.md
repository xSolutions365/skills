# Step 2 Workflow: Draft the internal team pack

## Objective

Hand the cast team the full context of the pitch and a plan that works with the people actually cast.

## Required actions

1. Draft `internal/team-handover-brief.md` from [../assets/templates/internal/team-handover-brief-template.md](../assets/templates/internal/team-handover-brief-template.md).
   - Tell the opportunity story in five to eight sentences: how it arrived, what the client wants, why us, and what winning looks like for the client and for CreateFuture.
   - Include the glossary and a who's-who covering both organisations.
   - Include the commercial ways of working: pricing model, time-logging expectations from week 0, and change-control triggers.
2. Draft `internal/expectation-register.md` from [../assets/templates/internal/expectation-register-template.md](../assets/templates/internal/expectation-register-template.md).
   - Add one row per stakeholder expectation, including every `DRIFT:` row.
   - Include technical and operational stakeholders even when the engagement is experience-led, because they often judge the final output.
   - For each row, state how we will meet it, reset it, or park it, and who owns that.
3. Draft `internal/plan-and-ownership.md` from [../assets/templates/internal/plan-and-ownership-template.md](../assets/templates/internal/plan-and-ownership-template.md).
   - Lay the SoW week-by-week plan out against each person's allocation, start date, and leave.
   - Suggest a lead and a backup for every week and every deliverable. When someone is away for a critical week, move the lead role to someone who is available and say so.
   - Treat casting as fixed. List only uncast roles, deliverables with no possible owner, or allocation shortfalls as `casting asks`.
4. Draft `internal/trade-offs-and-raid.md` from [../assets/templates/internal/trade-offs-and-raid-template.md](../assets/templates/internal/trade-offs-and-raid-template.md).
   - Frame known constraints as trade-offs chosen with open eyes, each with a fallback, a trigger date, and an owner.
   - Add open method, fidelity, and tooling choices as decisions, not as settled facts.
5. Draft `internal/growth-map.md` from [../assets/templates/internal/growth-map-template.md](../assets/templates/internal/growth-map-template.md), using `opportunity` and `stakeholder` rows. List the relationships to build and the signals to log during delivery.
   - Add the common follow-ons from the engagement shape guide as `DEFAULT` rows, even when the inputs don't mention them.
6. Use [engagement-shape-guide.md](engagement-shape-guide.md) for default risks and follow-ons when the ledger is thin, and mark those as `DEFAULT`.
7. Mark any missing or inferred fact `TO CONFIRM`, and add `LOW CONTEXT` to any section fed by a missing or partial input category.
8. Where availability is unknown, write `Lead: TO CONFIRM (candidate: <name>)`. Do not name a firm lead.
9. Write `internal/pitch-lead-questions.md` with every `TO CONFIRM` someone at CreateFuture can answer, grouped by pitch lead, account lead, and casting. When pitch or scope calls are missing, add the discovery questions from the missing data rules.
10. Write `internal/README.md` from [../assets/templates/internal/readme-template.md](../assets/templates/internal/readme-template.md): a reading guide by cast person, following [reading-guide-rules.md](reading-guide-rules.md), then the pack index, following the cross-reference rules. On first use in each file, name every ID and show its home file and section.

## Done when

- All five internal files, plus pitch-lead questions when needed, exist and each starts with the internal marker.
- Every SoW deliverable has a suggested lead in the plan.
- Every `DRIFT:` row appears in the expectation register.
- `internal/README.md` opens with a reading guide entry for every cast person, every uncast role, and the account lead.
