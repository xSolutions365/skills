# Reading Guide Rules

## Objective

Give every person on the cast team a short, personal reading list, so nobody has to read the whole pack to find the parts they own.

## Required actions

1. Write the reading guide at the top of `internal/README.md`, from [../assets/templates/internal/readme-template.md](../assets/templates/internal/readme-template.md). The file index goes below it.
2. Add one entry per cast person, by name and role, taken from casting. Add an entry for every uncast role, headed `Uncast: <role>`, so whoever joins late has a way in.
3. Add an entry for the account lead, and for the pitch lead when they are not cast, because they answer the pitch-lead questions.
4. Give each entry three parts:
   - `Read first`: two to four files or sections, in reading order, about 15 minutes in total.
   - `Skim`: up to three further files or sections.
   - `You own`: the deliverables, week leads, questions, risks, decisions, and prerequisite chases where this person is the named or candidate owner, each as a named ID with its home file on first use.
5. Start from the role defaults below, then adjust to what the ledger and plan say this person owns:
   - Product, strategy or engagement lead: the pitch-lead questions, the handover brief, and plan and ownership first; skim the expectation register, the RAID, and the client engagement plan.
   - Designer or researcher: the handover brief (story, who's who, glossary), the client research access plan, and the client kick-off agenda first; skim the expectation register and the client final playback skeleton.
   - Delivery manager: plan and ownership, the client prerequisites checklist, and the client ways of working first; skim the RAID and the handover brief's commercial ways of working.
   - Architect or engineer: the handover brief (the brief and who's who), the technical rows of the expectation register, and their deliverables in the client engagement plan first; skim the RAID.
   - Account lead: the pitch-lead questions and the growth map first; skim the handover brief and the expectation register.
6. Name every file with its folder, e.g. `client/research-access-plan.md › How we'll reach them, with fallbacks`, following the cross-reference rules.
7. Mark ownership that rests on a candidate lead `TO CONFIRM (candidate)`, matching plan and ownership.
8. Keep the guide internal. The client pack has no reading guide, because the client does not see casting.

## Done when

- Every cast person, every uncast role, and the account lead has an entry.
- Every `Read first` list fits in about 15 minutes, and every file and section it names exists.
- Every deliverable, week lead, and open decision appears under exactly one person's `You own`, or under an uncast role.
