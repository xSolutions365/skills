# project-kick-off

## Overview

Turns the material from a won engagement (the SoW or proposal, pitch and scope-call transcripts, internal notes, and casting) into two local draft packs. Commercial context never reaches the client pack. Anything the inputs don't support is marked `TO CONFIRM` or `LOW CONTEXT` rather than invented. A readiness verdict (`READY`, `READY WITH GAPS` or `HOLD`) tells you whether the client pack can be sent yet.

**Internal team pack:**
- **Start here (README):** a reading guide for each person as cast, with what to read first, what to skim, and what they own, followed by the file index.
- **Team handover brief:** the story so far, the client, the brief, who's who, a glossary, and commercial ways of working.
- **Expectation register:** what each stakeholder expects, including scope that moved after the proposal.
- **Plan and ownership:** the plan laid out against the team as cast, with leads, backups and casting asks.
- **Trade-offs and RAID:** constraints treated as choices, each with a fallback, plus open decisions.
- **Growth map:** adjacent opportunities, relationships to build, and a signals log.
- **Pitch-lead questions:** about 15 minutes of questions for the account lead, the pitch lead and casting.
- **Evidence ledger:** the source of every fact, context coverage, critical facts and validation results.

**Client kick-off pack:**
- **Kick-off agenda and deck content:** slide by slide, with questions to ask on the call.
- **Engagement plan:** objectives, success measures, the week-by-week plan, deliverables, RACI, and the choices to agree together.
- **Prerequisites checklist:** what we need from the client, with owners and needed-by dates.
- **Research access plan:** who we'll speak to, and a fallback ladder if we can't reach them.
- **Ways of working:** cadence, channels, how decisions are made, feedback, and scope changes.
- **Final playback skeleton:** built around the tangible outputs the final audience needs to see.

## When to use it

- Casting is confirmed and you're preparing the internal or client kick-off for a discovery, build, or strategic engagement.
- The cast team wasn't in the pitch and needs a fast, sourced handover.
- You want the prerequisites checklist sent to the client early, with dates worked back from week one.
- You want to catch scope that moved between the proposal and later calls before it catches you.
- You want the final playback shaped from day one.

## Example prompts

- "Use `project-kick-off` with this SoW, the three pitch transcripts, and the casting list. Kick-off is Friday."
- "Use `project-kick-off` to build the client prerequisites checklist and research access plan for this discovery sprint."
- "Use `project-kick-off` to write a handover brief for the team joining this build engagement next week."
